    Data

    Built in: ENTSO-E day-ahead prices for 32 SDAC zones, as daily baseload averages (time-weighted, CET delivery day). Italy uses the North zone (IT-NORD). To add newer days, run the Spotrank data builder and load the new spotrank_data.json here. A wide CSV also works: a date column followed by zone columns such as NO1, SE3, DE-LU in €/MWh. Loaded files stay in this browser, and your API key never goes into the page.

    Prices: SDAC day-ahead, €/MWh. Real data comes from the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform. Italy is shown as one zone. Switzerland (CH) is included although it runs its own day-ahead auction outside SDAC; Ireland (SEM) is left out.

    How to play

    • Each day you get one delivery day and five bidding zones from the SDAC day-ahead market.
    • Rank them by daily baseload price, the time-weighted average of every hour or quarter-hour of the delivery day. The cheapest goes at the top.
    • You have 4 attempts. After each one, ✓ green means right place (it locks in) and ✕ grey means wrong place.
    • Before you rank, the market briefing shows what drove each zone that day: wind and solar, demand, the main power source, imports or exports, and any congested border. No prices.
    • Practice rounds: a random day, or any day since 2021 you choose. They don't count on the leaderboard.
    • Every miss unlocks a clue with numbers: first the price range of the five, then a record or an unusual rank, then one zone's actual price.
    • Drag a zone to where it belongs and the others slide out of the way (on a phone, press and hold it first). You can also tap two zones to swap them, or use the arrow buttons. With a zone focused, ↑/↓ moves it and Enter selects it for a swap.
    • The daily puzzle is the same for everyone and counts toward your streak. Practice rounds don't count.
    • Forecast: tomorrow. After the daily puzzle, rank the same five zones by their price for the next day, once, before 11:00 CET (Switzerland's own auction closes then; the main auction at 12:00). You get a point for each zone in the right place; zones less than €1 apart count either way. Beat the naive forecast, which is the zones in the day before's order.
